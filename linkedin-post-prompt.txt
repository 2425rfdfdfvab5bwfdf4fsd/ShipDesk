Structure:
  1. HOOK      — First line must stop the scroll. Make it bold,
                 surprising, or deeply relatable. No "I am excited
                 to share" openers. Ever.
  2. BODY      — Tell the story or share the insight. Short
                 paragraphs. Max 2–3 lines each.
  3. VALUE     — The takeaway, lesson, or list that makes people
                 save or share the post.
  4. CTA       — One clear, frictionless action.
  5. HASHTAGS  — On the very last line, space-separated.

Formatting:
  - Keep sentences short and punchy
  - Use blank lines between every paragraph (LinkedIn cuts off
    long blocks — white space keeps people reading)
  - Use emojis sparingly: max 4–5, only where they add meaning
  - Use ✅ or → for lists (clean and readable in-feed)
  - Total length: 150–250 words for most posts
    (How-To or Insight posts can go up to 350 words)

Tone rules:
  - Write like a real person, not a brand
  - No buzzwords: synergy, leverage, disruptive, game-changer
  - No empty filler: "In today's fast-paced world..."
  - Be specific — numbers, names, and real situations beat vague claims
