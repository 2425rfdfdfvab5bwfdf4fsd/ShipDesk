# Indie Hackers Post — ShipDesk Launch

## Title
I was losing clients not because of bad work — but because of bad communication

---

## Body

Every Sunday night I'd sit down to write client update emails.

An hour later I'd have 3 sentences.

Not because the work wasn't good — but because translating GitHub commits into something a non-technical client understands is genuinely painful.

That was my real problem. Not the code. The communication.

So I built **ShipDesk**.

---

It connects to your GitHub, reads your week's commits and PRs, and generates a clean client-facing status report automatically. Your client gets a branded portal with their reports, invoices, files, and a way to message you — all in one place.

No more Sunday night emails. No more "where are we on this?" Slack pings.

**The product handles the update. You just do the work.**

---

I tried Notion, Basecamp, custom setups. None of them connected to my actual work or looked professional enough to send a client to.

ShipDesk does both — and it took me one setup to never write a status email again.

---

MVP is live. I'm looking for freelance developers to try it free and tell me what's missing.

Would love your honest feedback — especially if you've tried and failed with other tools.
